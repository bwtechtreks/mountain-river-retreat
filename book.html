First Time Staying in a Camper?

No worries — glamping is easy and comfortable. Here's what to know so you feel right at home.

💧

Water is Limited

The camper runs on tank water. It's plenty for your stay, but be mindful — take shorter showers and don't leave taps running. If you notice water running low, let us know.

🚽

The Toilet

Only flush toilet paper — nothing else (no wipes, paper towels, or hygiene products), as it drains to a holding tank. A small step or foot pedal operates the flush.

🔌

Power & Outlets

The camper is fully powered with standard outlets. Just avoid running too many high-draw appliances (space heaters, hair dryers) at the exact same time to prevent tripping a breaker.

🌡️

Heating & Cooling

Central AC and propane heat keep things comfortable year-round. Campers heat and cool quickly given the smaller space — give it a few minutes to adjust.

🚪

Doors & Steps

The entry has a step or two — watch your footing, especially at night. The door latches firmly; give it a solid pull to seal it against the weather and bugs.

🧺

Cozy by Design

A camper is a snug, efficient space — everything has its place. Storage is built into nooks and under seating. It's tidy living that keeps you close to the outdoors.

🐻

You're in the Mountains

Keep food sealed and trash secured — we're in wildlife country. Don't leave food out overnight, inside or out. Enjoy the critters from a respectful distance.

📵

Off the Grid

Cell service is limited. Download maps, music, and anything you need before you arrive. Consider it permission to truly unplug and relax.

❓

Questions? Just Ask

We leave a welcome guide inside with everything you need to know. First camper stay should be fun, not confusing — reach out anytime and we'll help.
