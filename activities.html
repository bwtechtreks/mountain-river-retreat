Nearby Adventures

The area is packed with outdoor activities — whether you want to relax by the water or hit the trails.

🎣

Fishing

Trout stocking right out front on the Middle Fork River. The river is regularly stocked — bring your rod and your WV fishing license.

🦌

Hunting

Excellent deer, turkey, and small game hunting in season. West Virginia is a hunter's paradise.

🏎️

ATV & Side-by-Side

A rider's basecamp. The driveway fits two vehicles plus a trailer, with plenty of nearby trails and organized local rides most weekends. Bring your own rig and explore the mountain roads. Please note: no driving on the grass or in the woods on the property.

🏊

Swimming

Natural swimming hole right on the property. Cool off in the river on summer days.

🌲

Audra State Park

Nearby park with hiking trails, swimming holes, and beautiful rock formations along the Middle Fork River — the same river that runs through the property.

💧

Blackwater Falls

One of WV's most iconic state parks. Stunning waterfalls, canyon views, and miles of trails.

🏞️

New River Gorge

America's newest National Park. World-class rock climbing, whitewater rafting, and hiking.

🚶

Hiking

Miles of trails through the Appalachian mountains — from easy riverside walks to challenging ridge climbs.

📵

Disconnect

Limited cell reception up on the mountain. Leave the noise behind and actually relax for once.
