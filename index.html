Your Mountain Hideaway

Tucked away in the hills near Ellamore, West Virginia, our glamping retreat sits on a full acre of waterfront property on the Middle Fork River. The river out front is home to a natural swimming hole and is a regular trout stocking site — bring your rod and your sense of adventure.

This is a true escape. Cell reception is limited up here on the mountain, and that's the point. Leave the noise behind, breathe the mountain air, and reconnect with what matters.

Whether you're here to fish, hunt, ride trails, or just sit by the fire and listen to the river — this is your basecamp.

Nearby Adventures

The area is packed with outdoor activities — whether you want to relax by the water or hit the trails.

🎣

Fishing

Trout stocking right out front on the Middle Fork River. The river is regularly stocked — bring your rod and your WV fishing license.

🦌

Hunting

Excellent deer, turkey, and small game hunting in season. West Virginia is a hunter's paradise.

🏎️

ATV & Side-by-Side

A rider's basecamp. The driveway fits two vehicles plus a trailer, with plenty of nearby trails and organized local rides most weekends. Bring your own rig and explore the mountain roads. Please note: no driving on the grass or in the woods on the property.

🏊

Swimming

Natural swimming hole right on the property. Cool off in the river on summer days.

🌲

Audra State Park

Nearby park with hiking trails, swimming holes, and beautiful rock formations along the Middle Fork River — the same river that runs through the property.

💧

Blackwater Falls

One of WV's most iconic state parks. Stunning waterfalls, canyon views, and miles of trails.

🏞️

New River Gorge

America's newest National Park. World-class rock climbing, whitewater rafting, and hiking.

🚶

Hiking

Miles of trails through the Appalachian mountains — from easy riverside walks to challenging ridge climbs.

📵

Know Before You Go

A few things to keep in mind so everyone has a great experience.

🚭

No Smoking

No smoking inside the retreat. You're welcome to smoke outdoors.

👟

No Shoes Inside

Please remove shoes before entering to keep things clean and cozy.

🔇

Quiet Hours

Follow local government quiet hours. Be respectful of the mountain and your neighbors.

🐕

Dogs Welcome

Dogs are okay with prior approval. Let us know when you inquire.

🚫

No Parties

This is a peaceful retreat, not a party spot. No events or large gatherings.

📱

Limited Cell Service

Cell reception is limited. Plan accordingly — download maps, let people know you'll be off-grid.
